# THE GUPTA AGE — The Golden Age of Ancient India
### *An Immersive 3D Virtual Digital Museum Portfolio (c. 320 – 550 CE)*

> *"Exploring the Empire, Science, Literature, Art and Culture of the Gupta Period"*

---

## 🏛️ Project Overview
**The Gupta Age** is a premium interactive 3D educational portfolio website constructed as a cinematic virtual museum dedicated to the golden age of ancient Indian civilization.

Built entirely with modern vanilla web standards (**HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Three.js, and GSAP**), it delivers a museum-grade experience without any backend, external frameworks, or build dependencies.

---

## 🏺 Key Features & Interactive Architecture

### 1. 🌟 Hero Section: Ancient Indian Temple Sanctum
- **3D Procedural Temple Environment:** Monolithic fluted sandstone columns with inverted bell/lotus capitals (*kalasam*), square abacus (*phalaka*), and carved architraves.
- **Volumetric Atmospheric Effects:** 1,800+ floating golden dust motes drifting in warm morning sunlight, dynamic exponential fog, and depth perspective.
- **Interactive Action:** Glowing antique-gold button **“ENTER THE ARCHIVE”** smoothly dives the 3D camera into the temple sanctum.
- **Procedural Soundscape:** Web Audio API synthesizer generating a meditative Tanpura drone with rich natural harmonics and Tibetan singing bowl / temple bell resonant chimes.

### 2. 🗺️ Section 1 — The Gupta Empire
- **Interactive 3D Ancient India Map:** Clickable territorial hubs (**Pāṭaliputra, Ujjayinī, Nālandā, Mathurā, Prayāga, Bharuch, Tāmralipti**) revealing imperial chronicles, trade links, and administrative roles.
- **Connected Timeline:** Chronology cards for Chandragupta I, Samudragupta, and Chandragupta II connected by glowing animated gold lines.
- **Interactive 3D Imperial Gold Dīnāra:** Standalone Three.js 3D coin viewer with full mouse/touch drag rotation, specular gold sheen, and a **Flip Coin 180°** button showcasing King Samudragupta with the bow and Garuda standard on the obverse, and Goddess Lakshmī on the reverse.

### 3. 👑 Section 2 — Great Rulers
- **Three Royal 3D Tilt Cards:**
  - **Chandragupta I:** Founder of Gupta imperial power, matrimonial alliance with the Licchavis, establishment of the Gupta Sanvat Era (319–320 CE).
  - **Samudragupta:** The "Indian Napoleon", Digvijaya conquests recorded on the Prayāga Prashasti, Kavirāja (King of Poets), and the Ashvamedha sacrifice.
  - **Chandragupta II (Vikramāditya):** Defeat of the Western Kshatrapas, patron of the Navaratnas (Nine Gems), and travel accounts of Chinese pilgrim Faxian.
- **Real-Time Perspective Tilt:** Dynamic holographic gold glare responding to cursor coordinates with expanding imperial dossiers.

### 4. 🔭 Section 3 — Science & Mathematics
- **Dark Astronomical Observatory:** Night sky with thousands of twinkling stars and planetary orbital rings.
- **Rotating 3D Astronomical Instrument (Golayantra):** Multi-ring armillary sphere featuring the outer meridian, celestial equator, 23.5° tilted ecliptic/zodiac band, and central spinning Earth globe.
- **Scholar Spotlight — Aryabhata:** Author of the *Āryabhaṭīya* (476–550 CE).
- **Interactive Calculators:**
  - **Aryabhata’s Pi (π) Calculator:** Interactive slider calculating circumference via Aryabhata's formula ($\pi \approx \frac{62832}{20000} = 3.1416$).
  - **Earth Axial Rotation Principle:** Demonstration of daily planetary spin and celestial shadow geometry.
  - **Zero (*Śūnya*) & Decimal Place Value:** *“Sthānāt sthānaṁ daśaguṇaṁ syāt”*.

### 5. 📜 Section 4 — Literature & Education
- **Ancient Manuscript Library Aesthetic:** Warm parchment tones, Sanskrit shlokas from Kālidāsa's *Meghadūtam*, and records of classical drama (*Abhijñānaśākuntalam*, *Mrichchhakatika*, *Panchatantra*).
- **Nālandā Mahāvihāra:** The world's first residential international university and its 9-storey library *Dharmagañja*.
- **Unrolling Palm-Leaf Scroll Modal:** Clicking **“Explore Ancient Scroll”** triggers a physics-based unrolling animation of an authentic cured palm-leaf (*Tāḍapatra*) folio with Brahmi calligraphy and English poetic translations.

### 6. 🗿 Section 5 — Art & Architecture (3D Gallery)
- **Interactive 3D Museum Gallery:**
  - **Sarnath Buddha:** Iconic Dharmachakra Pravartana Mudra with ornate carved halo (*prabhāmaṇḍala*).
  - **Mehrauli Iron Pillar:** 1,600-year rust-resistant metallurgical marvel with ancient Brahmi Sanskrit inscription.
  - **Dashavatara Temple Deogarh:** Classical Panchayatana stone temple with early curvilinear Shikhara spire.
  - **Palm-Leaf Manuscript Bundle (Tāḍapatra):** Bound stack of inscribed folios with silk binding cord.
  - **Royal Archer Gold Coin:** High-relief numismatic medal.
- **Full Controls:** 360° mouse/touch drag, auto-rotate toggle, zoom in/out, and curatorial plaque dossiers.

### 7. 💰 Section 6 — Economy & Trade
- **Ancient Marketplace Environment:** Trade routes across the Arabian Sea to Rome and Byzantium, and across the Bay of Bengal to Suvarṇabhūmi (Southeast Asia).
- **Merchant Guilds (*Śreṇīs*):** Autonomous banking and craft guilds.
- **Falling Gold Coins Effect:** Interactive **“Mint a Coin”** button that showers tumbling 3D gold Dīnāras with crystal chime audio effects.

### 8. 🪔 Section 7 — Religion & Culture
- **Harmonious Synthesis:** Pluralistic coexistence of Hindu traditions (Vaishnavism/Shaivism), Buddhism (Mahāyāna & Nālandā), and Jainism (Valabhī Council).
- **Temple Sanctum Visuals:** Warm diya oil lamp lighting with billowing volumetric incense smoke particle wisps.

### 9. ⚔ Section 8 — The Decline
- **Atmospheric Transition:** Environment shifts from bright gold to weathered bronze and dark slate.
- **Cracked Ancient Stone Texture:** Visual fissure overlays and an animated timeline detailing:
  1. Hūṇa Invasions (Toramāṇa & Mihirakula)
  2. Rise of Regional Feudatories (Maitrakas, Maukharis, Yaśodharman)
  3. Economic Pressures & Coin Debasement
  4. Dynastic Succession Crises

### 10. 🏛️ Section 9 — The Enduring Legacy
- **Five Glowing Monolith Pillars:** Monolithic stone pillars radiating vertical celestial light beams representing **SCIENCE, MATHEMATICS, LITERATURE, ART, and CULTURE**.
- **Grand Inscribed Statement:**
  > *“The Gupta Age continues to be remembered for its remarkable contributions to Indian civilization.”*

### 11. ✍ Final Section: Ancient Stone Inscription Panel
- **Monumental Epigraph Slab:** Carved with Brahmi inscription and English typography:
  *“THE GUPTA AGE • A Legacy Written in Stone • Explore • Learn • Preserve”*.
- **Interactive Charcoal Stone Rubbing:** Users drag/swipe over the sandstone slab to perform an authentic charcoal rubbing, revealing the glowing golden Brahmi characters from beneath ancient weathered dust.

## 🚀 How to Run the Website

### Option 1: Direct File Open
Simply double-click [`index.html`](file:///D:/nme%20portfolio/index.html) in your file explorer, or drag it into any modern web browser (Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, Mozilla Firefox, Safari).

### Option 2: Local Web Server
You can also run a static web server from the project directory:

**Using Python:**
```powershell
cd "D:\nme portfolio"
python -m http.server 8080
```
Then visit: `http://localhost:8080`

**Using Node.js:**
```powershell
cd "D:\nme portfolio"
npx serve .
```
